3D Rayquaza card.
  Laser cut 
  and assembled by my squirrels fingers. You can adjust the case by changing the hook sizes, and insert
  or remove layers however you want. Alternatives include a light up backing (sadly with only a makeshift
  masking tape diffuser), or make the back transparent with the red Pokémon cover. Stand is also lazercut.
  (PS: there isn't any green in the entire model, the apparent colour is exclusively additively colour-mixed
  from yellow and blue.)
Created as a birthday gift because why not.
  
Lasercut and Engraving; Clear, Red, Blue, Yellow Acrylic, 1/8" Plywood, 
Couple LEDs, and basic soldering for the backlight.

All dxf files designed on Fusion 360 and arranged out on Adobe Photoshop.
